Privacy Policy for Web Image Formatter

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Web Image Formatter is developed and provided by tekyeti ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how Web Image Formatter handles information.

Information we collect

Web Image Formatter does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information or usage data.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, user accounts, or third-party software development kits that collect data.

Images, PDFs, and exported files

Images and PDF files you choose are opened and processed locally on your Mac. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. The app writes exported images only to a folder you select.

Web Image Formatter does not retain copies of your source files or exported files. Your files remain under your control and are subject to the storage, backup, and synchronization services you choose to use on your Mac.

App preferences

Web Image Formatter stores app preferences locally on your Mac, including custom image templates, changes to built-in templates, and your selected template. This information is used only to provide the app's functionality and is not transmitted to us.

You can remove this locally stored information by deleting the app's data from your Mac or by uninstalling the app and removing its associated container data.

Network access

Web Image Formatter does not use an internet connection to process images and does not send data to our servers.

If you obtained the app through the Mac App Store, Apple may process information related to downloading, purchasing, updating, diagnostics, and use of the App Store. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Web Image Formatter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will include a new effective date. If an update materially changes how information is handled, we will provide notice through the app, its distribution page, or another appropriate method.
