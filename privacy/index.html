Privacy Policy for Web Image Formatter

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Web Image Formatter is developed and provided by tekyeti ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how Web Image Formatter handles information.

Information we collect

Web Image Formatter does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information or usage data.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, user accounts, or third-party software development kits that collect data.

Images, PDFs, and exported files

Images and PDF files you choose are opened and processed locally on your Mac. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. The app writes exported images only to a folder you select.

Web Image Formatter does not retain copies of your source files or exported files. Your files remain under your control and are subject to the storage, backup, and synchronization services you choose to use on your Mac.

App preferences

Web Image Formatter stores app preferences locally on your Mac, including custom image templates, changes to built-in templates, and your selected template. This information is used only to provide the app's functionality and is not transmitted to us.

You can remove this locally stored information by deleting the app's data from your Mac or by uninstalling the app and removing its associated container data.

Network access

Web Image Formatter does not use an internet connection to process images and does not send data to our servers.

If you obtained the app through the Mac App Store, Apple may process information related to downloading, purchasing, updating, diagnostics, and use of the App Store. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Web Image Formatter does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will include a new effective date. If an update materially changes how information is handled, we will provide notice through the app, its distribution page, or another appropriate method.

QR Kiosk

Privacy Policy for QR Kiosk

Effective date: October 8, 2026

TekYeti ("we," "us," or "our") provides the QR Kiosk application for iPadOS. This Privacy Policy explains how QR Kiosk handles information.

Summary

QR Kiosk does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking technology. QR Kiosk does not transmit camera images to TekYeti. The app scans a QR code on the device and opens the web address contained in that code.

Camera and QR codes

QR Kiosk requests access to the iPad camera solely to detect and read QR codes. Camera images are processed on the device and are not stored or sent to TekYeti.

When QR Kiosk recognizes a QR code containing an HTTP or HTTPS web address, it uses that address to load the corresponding website. The address and the ordinary information needed for an internet connection are consequently sent to the website being opened and its service providers, not to TekYeti unless TekYeti operates that website.

Websites opened in QR Kiosk

Websites displayed by QR Kiosk are separate services. Their operators may receive information normally provided during a web request, such as the requested address, IP address, device or browser information, cookies, and information a user submits to the website. A website may also use cookies, local storage, analytics, or other technologies according to its own privacy policy.

TekYeti does not control and is not responsible for the privacy, security, content, or data practices of third-party websites opened from QR codes. Administrators and users should scan only QR codes from trusted sources and review the privacy policy of each website they use.

Information stored on the iPad

QR Kiosk may store the following information locally on the iPad:

  • Whether the one-time Settings instructions have already been displayed.
  • An optional Settings PIN, stored using the iPadOS Keychain.

This locally stored information is used only to operate the app. QR Kiosk does not send it to TekYeti.

Information we collect

QR Kiosk itself does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or camera recordings. We do not use information from QR Kiosk to track users across apps or websites.

If an organization configures QR Kiosk to open a website it owns or manages, that organization’s collection and use of information through the website is governed by its own privacy policy and administrative practices.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information collected through QR Kiosk. Because QR Kiosk itself does not collect personal information, we do not share such information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data retention and deletion

QR Kiosk does not maintain a TekYeti account or server-side profile for users. The local instruction status and optional Settings PIN remain on the iPad until changed, deleted through the app where applicable, or removed by deleting the app or erasing the device.

The embedded browser used to display scanned websites uses a temporary, non-persistent storage session rather than a saved browsing session. Cookies, cache, and local storage created while a website is open remain available only while that website stays on screen (for example, while an administrator opens Settings and returns to it). When an administrator chooses to scan a new code, this browsing session and all associated website data are discarded from the device. Website operators may separately retain information they received according to their own privacy policies. Requests concerning information collected by a website should be directed to that website’s operator.

Children’s privacy

QR Kiosk is not designed to collect personal information from children. The content and age suitability of websites opened by QR codes are controlled by the QR codes provided and the operators of those websites. Administrators are responsible for limiting QR codes and web content appropriately for their users.

Security

We use platform security features, including the iPadOS Keychain for the optional Settings PIN. No method of device storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and the security of a website displayed in QR Kiosk depends on that website and its operator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when QR Kiosk’s features or legal obligations change. We will revise the effective date when an update is published.
