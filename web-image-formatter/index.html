Description (max 4000)

Web Image Formatter makes it quick to get images and PDFs ready for the web.

Choose your files, pick a template, and export. Templates define the output size and format you need, so you get consistent results every time. Use the built-in templates, adjust them, or create your own. The app remembers your selected template.

FEATURES
- Open images and PDF files
- Built-in and custom image templates
- Export to any folder you choose
- Fast, native Mac app
- Works completely offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your files are processed locally on your Mac and are never uploaded. There are no ads, analytics, tracking, or accounts. Web Image Formatter does not collect any data.

Questions? Contact support@tekyeti.dev.
