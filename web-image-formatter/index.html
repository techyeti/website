Description (max 4000)

Web Image Formatter makes it quick to prepare consistent, web-ready images on your Mac.

Import images or PDFs, choose a reusable output template, adjust the crop, and export polished files at the dimensions and file-size limits you need. Subject-aware positioning helps keep the important part of an image in frame, while batch export makes it easy to process a full gallery at once.

Features include:

- Reusable built-in and custom image templates
- Precise output dimensions and maximum file sizes
- JPEG, HEIC, and PNG export
- Subject-aware cropping and positioning
- Manual zoom, offset, rotation, and flip controls
- Batch import and export
- Drag-and-drop support
- Local, private processing with no uploads or accounts

Web Image Formatter is designed for teams and individuals who regularly prepare product, editorial, marketing, or catalog images for the web.

App Review notes
